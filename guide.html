What n8n can automate in 30 minutes

n8n is a visual automation tool: you connect apps ("nodes") so data flows

between them without code. The three most common patterns small businesses

start with:

1. **Trigger → action**: a form submission, webhook or schedule starts the

flow; one or more apps receive the data.

2. **Polling → sync**: on a schedule, pull records from one system and push

them into another (e.g., orders → spreadsheet).

3. **Condition → branch**: if a record matches a rule, send it down one path

(e.g., high-value lead → Slack alert), otherwise another.

Worked example (described, not delivered)

*Problem:* form responses arrive in your inbox as emails and never reach your

CRM.

Approach:

1. Trigger on the form's webhook (or email).

2. Parse the fields (name, email, company, message).

3. Check for duplicates in the CRM.

4. Create or update the contact.

5. Send a confirmation to the customer and a notification to you.

That is the whole pattern. The PRAXOVEN certified workflow automates steps

2–5 with error handling and retries — delivered, installed and monitored by

us. This guide deliberately stops here: the working automation is the paid

outcome.
